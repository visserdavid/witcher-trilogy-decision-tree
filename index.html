Route planner

Click "I'm here" on a card once you reach that moment in the game. Only then do you see the options, and only after choosing do you see the consequences. Your choices carry over: what you pick in the first game goes on to the second, and the second goes on to the third. The page remembers everything in your browser.

A word of warning up front. Sources usually talk about five or six "major" choices, but the game branches less than it seems. Nearly every choice changes who stands beside or against you and which reward you get, not how the story ends.

What does The Witcher 2 actually carry over?

The Witcher 2 can import a final save from the first game. But its influence is far smaller than the marketing suggested at the time. It is mostly about gear and a handful of references, not different storylines.

Note: players also report inconsistencies, such as a book calling Adda dead while she is alive in the import, or an import wrongly assuming the Order's path. Choices from the first game do not count in The Witcher 3; it only reads a save from the second game.

Unlike the first game, this one truly splits. After chapter 1 you play an entirely different second chapter, in a different place and with different people. To see both halves, you have to play the game twice. Cards from the path you are not playing are faded out.

An honest warning about the sources: many guides contradict each other on long-term consequences. Only what appears in at least one solid guide and is not contradicted by another is included here. Where that was not possible, it says so.

The bridge to The Witcher 3

The Witcher 3 can import your save from the second game. Without a save, Morvran Voorhis asks you five questions in Vizima that settle the same things. The questions themselves give away a lot, so only open this section once you have finished the second game.

Show the five points that count (spoilers)
Choice in game 2Consequence in game 3Weight
Aryan La Valette spared or killedOnly a few lines of dialogue from one character.Small
With Roche or with IorvethOnly some dialogue with a character you meet later.Small
Triss rescued, or Anaïs or Saskia insteadTriss lives either way, but without special feelings for Geralt. Anaïs and Saskia do not appear in the third game.Small
Síle de Tansarville saved or notIf she lives, she has a small role in one quest late in the game.Small
Letho spared or killedIf he lives, you get an extra quest and he plays a part in later main quests. By default he is dead.Large

According to players who inspected the import file, an import reads one extra point: whether Radovid has Anaïs. Henselt's fate is not in it, even though some guides claim it carries over. So don't count on that.

Hearts of Stone and Blood and Wine are listed separately, at the bottom.

A tip from a sparring partner: in this game especially, it pays not to read ahead. The moments that decide Ciri's fate work best if you choose the way Geralt would react in that situation. Playing with a cheat sheet turns a father-daughter story into a points tally.
